DeltaFront Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information DeltaFront collects, how that information is used, how long certain information may be retained, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

DeltaFront collects information necessary to operate accounts, multiplayer games, profiles, leaderboards, clans, inventory, virtual Credits, security systems, and related services.

Account information

Depending on how you sign in, DeltaFront may receive account information from supported identity providers.

When you use Discord sign-in, DeltaFront may receive your Discord user identifier, username, display name, avatar information, and verified email address when Discord provides it.

When you use Google sign-in, DeltaFront may receive your Google account identifier, display name, profile image, email address, and information indicating whether that email address is verified.

If passwordless email sign-in is enabled, DeltaFront may collect the email address you submit in order to send a temporary sign-in link. DeltaFront does not use password-based authentication for this feature.

Guest accounts

DeltaFront may create a guest account when you use the Service without linking Discord, Google, or another supported sign-in method. Guest accounts may still store gameplay statistics, Credits, and inventory information.

Gameplay and account data

DeltaFront may store information including:

  • public player identifiers;
  • usernames and profile information;
  • game participation and game results;
  • wins, losses, and gameplay statistics;
  • leaderboard information;
  • Credits balances and transaction history;
  • virtual items and inventory ownership;
  • clan membership, roles, applications, and clan activity;
  • moderation status and bans;
  • security and audit records.

Technical and security information

DeltaFront may process IP addresses, request metadata, browser or device information, session identifiers, timestamps, server logs, rate-limit data, and other technical information needed to operate and secure the Service.

2. Cookies and Sessions

DeltaFront uses a refresh-session cookie named df_refresh to keep users signed in.

This cookie is used for authentication and session management. In production it is configured with security protections such as HttpOnly, Secure, and appropriate SameSite settings.

DeltaFront may also use short-lived cookies or browser state during OAuth sign-in and other security-sensitive flows.

3. Cloudflare Turnstile

DeltaFront uses Cloudflare Turnstile to help detect automated abuse and protect multiplayer admission and other security-sensitive operations.

When Turnstile is used, Cloudflare may process technical information necessary to determine whether a request appears legitimate. This may include IP address, browser information, and other security signals.

Cloudflare processes this information under its own privacy policies and terms.

4. How We Use Information

DeltaFront uses collected information to:

  • authenticate users and maintain sessions;
  • operate multiplayer games;
  • maintain player profiles and statistics;
  • calculate leaderboards;
  • award and spend Credits;
  • maintain virtual inventory and cosmetic ownership;
  • operate clans and clan membership features;
  • detect cheating, fraud, abuse, and security threats;
  • enforce bans and other moderation decisions;
  • investigate technical failures;
  • protect DeltaFront users and infrastructure.

5. Public Information

Some DeltaFront information is intentionally public. This may include usernames, public player identifiers, public profile statistics, leaderboard rankings, clan membership, clan names, clan tags, and other information intended to appear within the game.

DeltaFront does not intentionally expose private authentication credentials, provider secrets, refresh tokens, private database identifiers, or account email addresses through public player profiles, leaderboards, or clan pages.

6. Third-Party Sign-In Providers

Discord and Google authentication involves sending users to those providers and receiving information back after successful authorization.

DeltaFront requests only the scopes needed for authentication and basic account identification.

Your use of Discord or Google is also subject to those companies' privacy policies and terms.

7. Passwordless Email Sign-In

If email sign-in is enabled, DeltaFront generates a temporary, single-use magic-link token.

The raw token is sent through the configured email provider and is not stored in the DeltaFront database. DeltaFront stores a cryptographic hash of the token so that the link can be verified when used.

Magic-link tokens expire after a short period and cannot be reused after successful sign-in.

8. Data Retention

DeltaFront retains different types of data for different periods based on operational, security, gameplay, and integrity needs.

Active account information may be retained while the account exists. Session records may be removed after they expire and are no longer needed.

Stored detailed game records may be retained for up to approximately 60 days for game processing, troubleshooting, security, and abuse investigation, after which those detailed records may be pruned.

Aggregated statistics, anonymized historical data, transaction records, administrative audit records, and security information may be retained longer when needed to preserve system integrity, prevent abuse, or meet legal requirements.

Expired magic-link records may be removed after they are no longer needed for security and rate-limiting purposes.

9. Account Deletion

DeltaFront provides an account-deletion process for eligible accounts.

When an account is deleted, public profile access is removed and account data may be deleted or anonymized according to DeltaFront's data lifecycle rules.

Certain records may be retained or anonymized where necessary for security, fraud prevention, abuse investigation, transaction integrity, auditing, legal compliance, or preserving historical game integrity.

Detailed stored game records containing player names may remain for up to the normal game-record retention period before being pruned.

10. Security

DeltaFront uses technical safeguards intended to reduce unauthorized access and abuse. These include authenticated sessions, signed tokens, server-side authorization checks, encrypted network connections, rate-limiting, bot detection, restricted administrative tools, and database access controls.

No online service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Service Providers

DeltaFront may use third-party infrastructure and service providers to operate the Service, including:

  • DigitalOcean for server infrastructure;
  • Cloudflare for DNS, proxying, TLS, and Turnstile;
  • Discord for optional authentication;
  • Google for optional authentication;
  • an email delivery provider such as Resend or Postmark if email sign-in is enabled;
  • GitHub for publication of DeltaFront source code.

These providers may process information necessary to provide their services under their own privacy terms.

12. Children's Privacy

DeltaFront is not intended to knowingly collect personal information in violation of applicable children's privacy laws. Users must comply with any age requirements applicable to them and to the third-party account providers they use.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as DeltaFront's features and practices change. Material updates will be reflected by changing the effective date above.

14. Contact

Questions about privacy, account deletion, or this Privacy Policy may be sent to: privacy@deltafront.lol
